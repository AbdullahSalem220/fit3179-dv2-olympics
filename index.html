  • Olympedia, OlyMADMen. Medal tallies per Games, Summer 1896–2024 and Winter 1924–2026.

  • Paris 2024 Olympic Summer Games, Petro Ivaniuk (Kaggle, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Medals, medallists and athletes.
  • Olympics-Dataset, Keith Galli (MIT), from Olympedia. Athletes' birthplaces.
  • Australia at the Olympics, Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Cross-check of tallies and ranks.
  • Population, total (SP.POP.TOTL), World Bank (CC BY 4.0). Country populations, 2024.
  • National, state and territory population, March 2026, Australian Bureau of Statistics (CC BY 4.0).
  • GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Birthplace coordinates.
  • Natural Earth (public domain) via world-atlas (ISC). Map outlines and host-city locations.
  • country-codes, datasets.io (PDDL). Olympic to ISO country codes.
